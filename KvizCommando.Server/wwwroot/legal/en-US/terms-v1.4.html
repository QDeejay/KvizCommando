KvizCommando processes personal data to create and operate invitation-only accounts, authenticate users, send system emails, operate the game, maintain security, prevent abuse, provide support and comply with legal obligations.

3. Categories of data processed

  • Invitation, account and authentication: internal user identifier, email address, secure password hash, email-confirmation status, creation time, security stamps, failed login count, lockout data and two-factor-authentication data.
  • Profile: display name, preferred language, team name and avatar identifier.
  • Contact and billing details: phone number, billing name and address provided by the user; these are stored separately using AES-GCM encryption.
  • Game data: player identifier, rank, experience, credits, vouchers, characters, team, loadouts, questions, answers and statistics.

  • Security audit: event, outcome, UTC timestamp, internal actor and subject identifiers, request identifier, changed fields, document version and, where enabled, a keyed HMAC hash of the IP address.
  • Registration benefit: a non-reversible HMAC fingerprint of the normalised email address and the next date on which starting credits may be granted.
  • Email delivery: recipient address, sender, subject, system-message content, delivery operation identifier and status.
  • External login: if Facebook login is enabled, provider name, external-account identifier, email address, and tokens and expiry data required for the integration.
  • Browser storage: the cookies described below, selected language, application settings, viewed-help state, notification state and short-lived caches.

A payment data model is technically prepared, but there is currently no purchase or active payment-data processing. KvizCommando does not store full payment-card numbers or CVV/CVC codes.

6. Retention

  • Account, profile and game data is retained while the account exists or until deletion.

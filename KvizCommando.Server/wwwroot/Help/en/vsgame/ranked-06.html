Captain round and final result

After the normal rounds, every player becomes the questioner once. The other players – and the questioner as well – answer the selected captain question.

Who chooses first?The player currently in last place starts the captain round, while the leader takes the final turn.
Which questions are available?When it is your turn, you see only questions you did not use in the normal rounds. Clicking a card locks your choice. The selected question is highlighted briefly, then answering begins.
{{VS_CAPTAIN_MULTIPLIER}}× point valueEvery question point is doubled: correct and wrong answers, the questioner's result and the per-question speed point all use {{VS_CAPTAIN_POINT_UNIT}}-point units. There is no separate round-winner point, character XP or vitality loss. At the end of the captain round, the best combined time receives the speed bonus starting at {{VS_CAPTAIN_POINT_UNIT}} points; the questioner's own-question time is excluded from that total.

Normal-round flow

Every normal round starts with a numerical estimate, followed by every player taking a turn as questioner.

1. Numerical estimate – {{VS_GUESS_SECONDS}} secThe closest estimate earns +{{VS_POINT_UNIT}} point. Equal errors are ordered by the faster estimate. This creates the questioner order.
▶
2. Questions – {{VS_QUESTION_SECONDS}} secEvery player asks the category question assigned to this round. A submitted answer is final.
Responder: correct +{{VS_POINT_UNIT}}, wrong −{{VS_POINT_UNIT}}, no answer 0 points.
Fastest correct answer: the sole best modified time earns another +{{VS_POINT_UNIT}} point. If the best time is tied, that point increases the combined speed bonus; it does not increase when nobody answers correctly.
Questioner: keeps more points when fewer opponents answer correctly. Their own wrong or missing answer causes negative points.
End of round: the round winner earns another +{{VS_POINT_UNIT}} point. The player with the best combined round time receives the full speed bonus, which starts at 1 point and grows with every unawarded speed point. The original round order remains unchanged; a green arrow shows when the speed bonus would move a player higher by round points.

Time modifiers: active characters in a normal round can modify answer time according to the question category. Time modifiers – including Time Freeze – are used only to decide the bonus for the fastest correct answer: the displayed modified times are compared for this purpose. The combined round time includes the numeric-guess time and the unmodified time charged for answers to other players' questions; a questioner's own-question time is excluded. A wrong answer after using Time Freeze adds {{VS_WRONG_ANSWER_TIME_PENALTY_SECONDS}} seconds to the combined round time. At round end, character XP may be earned according to placement.
Vitality: characters get tired during normal rounds. A character loses 1 vitality when it is their turn to ask a question. They lose another 1 vitality after an incorrect answer to another player's question; skipping an answer does not reduce vitality. If a character's vitality reaches zero during a question, they may finish that question. From the next normal question onward, they cannot answer or use help. The numerical estimate and the captain round do not consume vitality. In a four-player match, a character can lose at most 4 vitality in one normal round: 1 for their own question and another 1 for each incorrect answer to the other three questions.

Details and deletion

Select a question, then use one of the buttons at the bottom of the screen.

Binoculars – full detailsShows the category, complete question, four answers and the results of your own question. The correct answer is the first, highlighted answer.
Tools – delete actionOpens the deletion confirmation. You can still go back here.
Trash – delete immediatelyAppears after selecting “Do not show anymore”. Pressing it deletes without asking again.
Question askedHow many times you asked this question of the other players.
Answered correctlyHow many times the other players answered correctly when you asked it.
RatioThe percentage of correct answers; it shows the success of your question after 10 asks.
Deletion cannot be undone.The question slot becomes empty and the question can no longer be used as your own question. Excess “Own question” entries in the Loadout are cleared automatically.
F1 – open help again

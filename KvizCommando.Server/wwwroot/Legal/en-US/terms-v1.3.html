KvizCommando processes personal data only for specified purposes, to the extent necessary and for no longer than required. 3. Categories of data processed

  • Account and authentication: internal user identifier, email address, secure password hash (the password itself is not readable), email-confirmation status, creation time, security stamps, failed login count, lockout data and two-factor-authentication status.
  • Profile: display name and its normalised form, preferred communication language, team name and its normalised form, and avatar identifier.
  • Contact and billing details: phone number, billing name, postal code, city and address lines one and two. These are stored separately using AES-GCM encryption.
  • Payment metadata: payment provider, token, card brand, last four digits, expiry and default status. Full card numbers and CVV/CVC codes are not stored; payment functionality is currently inactive.
  • Game data: player identifier, rank, experience, credits, vouchers, characters, team, loadouts, question slots, custom and pending questions, category, orientation, team and answer statistics, and session data required to operate the game.
  • Consent and legal records: accepted Terms version, acceptance time, marketing-consent status and changes; acceptance may also be supported by keyed hashes of the IP address and user-agent string.
  • Security audit: event and outcome, UTC timestamp, internal actor and subject identifiers, request identifier, changed fields, document version and, when enabled, a keyed HMAC hash of the IP address.
  • Registration benefit: a non-reversible HMAC fingerprint of the normalised email address and the date on which a new starting-credit benefit becomes available.
  • External login: where Facebook login is enabled, provider name, linked external account identifier, email address, and tokens and expiry information required for the integration.
  • Client-side data: essential authentication cookie, selected language, settings, viewed help pages and a short-lived language cache in browser localStorage or sessionStorage.

4. Purposes and legal bases

  • Performance of a contract: registration, login, account, profile, gameplay, leaderboard, statistics, credits and user-question management.
  • Legal obligation: accounting or regulatory duties and the necessary proof of consents and contractual acceptance.
  • Legitimate interests: information security, abuse and fraud prevention, troubleshooting, session protection, preventing repeated unauthorised use of the registration benefit and handling legal claims.
  • Consent: optional marketing communications. Consent may be withdrawn at any time without affecting processing carried out before withdrawal.

5. Sources of data

Most data is provided by the user or generated while the game is used. Where external login is enabled, the provider may supply an identifier and email address. Security and technical data is generated when the client communicates with the server.

6. Retention

  • The authentication cookie has a default lifetime of up to 7 days and may renew while used; logout or a security-stamp change may invalidate it.
  • Password, email and confirmation tokens have a default lifetime of 24 hours.
  • Security audit files are retained for 30 days by default.
  • The registration-benefit fingerprint prevents a repeated starting-credit benefit for 30 days. An expired record may technically remain until database maintenance, but no longer restricts eligibility; a later deletion or email change resets the date to 30 days from the new event.

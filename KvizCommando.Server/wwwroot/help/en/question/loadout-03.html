Choose and save

Changing the loadout takes three simple steps.

1Open a row
Click the topic you want to change.
2Choose a topic
Click the new topic in the list.
3Save it
You can change several rows before saving.
Floppy – save LoadoutThis is the actual save icon. The button is available only after you change at least one row. Press it to keep the new setup.

If you leave the screen without saving, the changes made here are not kept.

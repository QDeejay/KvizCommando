LOADOUT // 02 / 03

Own questions in the loadout

You can ask your accepted questions during play, but only in the loadout rows marked for this purpose.

Person raising a handThis is the actual icon marking rows in which the “Own question” option can be selected.
1
Only in the upper rowsOwn question cannot be selected in the second half of the loadout.
2
Accepted questions are requiredYou can use no more Own question rows than the number of accepted questions you have.
3
Half of the loadout is the maximumYour own questions cannot occupy more than half of the loadout, even if you have more accepted questions.
Example: with 8 loadout rows and 3 accepted questions, the icon appears next to the first 3 rows.

If you later delete one of your questions, excess “Own question” entries that are no longer usable are cleared automatically.

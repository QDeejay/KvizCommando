NEW QUESTION // 02 / 02

Keep it short, accurate and clear

Write a question that players can understand on their first reading and that has exactly one unquestionably correct answer.

01 // One clear questionDo not combine several separate questions in one sentence, and avoid ambiguous wording.
02 // Verified factsCheck names, dates, facts and the correct answer before submitting.
03 // Concise wordingDo not deliberately stretch the question. Unnecessary filler only makes it harder to read.
04 // Plausible answersThe three incorrect answers should fit the topic, but none of them may also be correct.
05 // Correct languageCheck spelling, punctuation and make every sentence easy to read.
06 // Lasting contentWhen possible, avoid questions whose correct answer may change in the near future.
Do not submit: offensive or vulgar text, personal data, deliberately misleading claims or invented facts.

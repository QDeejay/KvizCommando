VS LEADERBOARD // 01 / 01

All-time team standings

Teams are ranked by their best result in a ranked VS battle. The leaderboard shows team names.

All time – each team's best ranked battle score counts; scores from earlier battles are not added together.
Position – a higher score ranks first; the shorter time breaks a tie on points.
Leaderboard rows – the top ten positions and your team's position are shown. Your row is highlighted. The three dots mark positions omitted from the list.
Red time – shows how much longer the lower-ranked result took than the result immediately ahead of it with the same score. Differences above 99.9 seconds appear as +99.9s.

Daily, weekly and monthly leaderboards: not available yet.

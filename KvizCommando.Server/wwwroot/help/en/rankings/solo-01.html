SOLO LEADERBOARD // 01 / 01

Solo standings

Use the buttons on the left to switch between the overall, category game and orientation game leaderboards.

Overall – combines the points from your best category and orientation game results.
Category game and orientation game – separate standings for the two game types.
Position – a higher score ranks first; the shorter time breaks a tie on points.
Leaderboard rows – the top ten positions and your own position are shown. Your row is highlighted. The three dots mark positions omitted from the list.
Red time – shows how much longer the lower-ranked result took than the result immediately ahead of it with the same score. Differences above 99.9 seconds appear as +99.9s.

RECRUITING // 01 / 05

Choosing a new team member

The upper navigation lists the vacant team positions for which candidates are available. The selected position determines the new team member's primary orientation.

01
Choose a team positionUse the upper row to choose the primary orientation you need.
02
Review the candidatesEach card summarises the candidate's name, secondary orientation and three favourable categories.
03
Select someoneSelecting a card displays the candidate's portrait and enables the Hire button.
04
Check the detailsThe Hire button shows the candidate's favourable and unfavourable modifiers. Recruitment happens only after confirmation.
If a vacant position is temporarily unavailable, the upper navigation shows when it can be used again.

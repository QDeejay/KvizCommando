TEAM OVERVIEW // 02 / 06

Actions for team members

When an action becomes available, its name appears on the right side of the member's row.

PROMOTE
Promotion: Becomes available with sufficient XP and team level. It normally costs {{TEAM_PROMOTION_COST}} Tp; promotion to a new rank class is free.
RETIRE
Retirement: A member who has reached the end of their normal progression leaves the team permanently. The team receives {{TEAM_RETIRE_REWARD}} Tp.
FIRE
Dismissal: A fully exhausted member leaves the team permanently. No new candidate arrives for that position for {{TEAM_FIRE_RECRUIT_DELAY_DAYS}} day.
CHARGE
Restore vitality: A fully exhausted member's vitality can be restored to its maximum. This costs {{TEAM_HEAL_COST}} of the character's own development points.
DEVELOP
Character development: The member has an available personal development point. Selecting the row opens the character sheet.
At 0 vitality, a team member cannot be developed, promoted or retired. Only restoring vitality or dismissal is available. Ranks and rank classes are explained in detail in the character help.

Actions for team members

PROMOTE
PromotionRequires XP and the appropriate team level. Costs {{TEAM_PROMOTION_COST}} Tp; changing rank class is free.
RETIRE
RetirementThe member leaves after completing progression; the team receives {{TEAM_RETIRE_REWARD}} Tp.
FIRE
DismissalThe exhausted member leaves. No new candidate is available for {{TEAM_FIRE_RECRUIT_DELAY_DAYS}} day.
CHARGE
Restore vitalityRestores an exhausted member to maximum vitality for {{TEAM_HEAL_COST}} personal Sp.
DEVELOP
Character developmentRequires spendable personal Sp.
At zero vitality, only recovery or dismissal is available.

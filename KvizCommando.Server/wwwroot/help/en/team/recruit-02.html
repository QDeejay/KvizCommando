Secondary orientationThe abbreviation beside the name identifies it. The character's secondary modifiers come from this orientation.
Three categoriesThe categories of the primary, secondary and extra favourable modifiers.
SelectionSelecting the card displays the portrait and the Hire button on the left.
Primary modifiersThey come from the primary orientation fixed by the team position.
Secondary modifiersThey come from the candidate's secondary orientation.
Extra modifiersThey are associated with the character's sex.

TEAM OVERVIEW // 01 / 06

Your team's status

The upper panel shows your team's current progress and capacity.

OrganizationA higher team Lvl also means a higher organization level.
Lvl and XPLvl is the team's current level. XP shows its progress towards the next level.
Team development points – TpThe team's shared development points. They are used for promotions and improving helps.
BonusThe percentage bonus belonging to the team's current level.
Winner compensation – W. comp.Based on team level, a ×1.0–×3.0 multiplier increases the winner's leaderboard result. It does not change net points or rewards. At Lvl-0 it is N/A because ranked matches are not yet available.
Team sizeThe first number is the current number of members; the second is the largest team currently allowed.
Team membersThe member list and available actions.
HelpsDevelopment of the team's in-game helps.

!An action is available or required for that team member.

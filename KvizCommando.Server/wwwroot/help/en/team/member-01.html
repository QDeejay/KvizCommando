TEAM MEMBERS // 01 / 05

The selected team member

The upper navigation shows the occupied team positions. Select an item to open that member's profile.

RankThe team member's current military rank.
Rank classA group containing several consecutive ranks.
OrientationThe name of the orientation assigned to the team member.
XP required for the next rankShows how much more XP is needed before the next promotion.
Skill points – SpPoints that can be spent on the member's secondary and extra modifiers.
VitalityThe bar below the portrait shows the current and maximum values. The maximum increases with rank.
PensionCredits accumulated by the team member during service. When the member retires from service, the displayed credit amount is added to the player's balance.
Orientations and their structure are explained in the Recruitment help.

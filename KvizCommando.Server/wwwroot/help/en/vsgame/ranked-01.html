Assemble your battle team

You need at least {{VS_ENTRY_READY_COUNT}} battle-ready characters to open this menu. Matches always use the separately saved battle team.

Selection
Click a card to add or remove that character from the current lineup.
Classification indicators
The I–V lamps immediately show which battlefields accept the current lineup.
Green check: the character belongs to the current lineup.
Red X: the character cannot enter battle now. This can be caused by zero vitality, pending retirement or a rank restriction.
Save: becomes available after a change when the lineup qualifies for at least one battle classification.

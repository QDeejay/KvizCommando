Normal-round flow

Every normal round starts with a numerical estimate, followed by every player taking a turn as questioner.

1. Numerical estimate – {{VS_GUESS_SECONDS}} secThe closest estimate earns +{{VS_POINT_UNIT}} point. Equal errors are ordered by the faster estimate. This creates the questioner order.
▶
2. Questions – {{VS_QUESTION_SECONDS}} secEvery player asks the category question assigned to this round. A submitted answer is final.
Responder: correct +{{VS_POINT_UNIT}}, wrong −{{VS_POINT_UNIT}}, no answer 0 points.
Fastest correct answer: the best modified time earns another +{{VS_POINT_UNIT}} point. If the two best times are equal, nobody receives that point immediately; it is added to the speed bonus awarded at the end of the round. The bonus does not grow when nobody answers correctly.
Questioner: the questioner answers as well. Each opponent's wrong answer adds +{{VS_POINT_UNIT}} to the question value, each skipped answer adds +2, and each correct answer adds 0. A correct answer gives the questioner that value; a wrong answer loses the same amount. Skipping loses the normal maximum.
End of round: the player with the most round points earns another +{{VS_POINT_UNIT}} point. The player with the best combined round time receives the speed bonus, which starts at 1 point and grows when question-level speed points remain unawarded because of ties. A green arrow shows when this bonus moves the player higher in the round's point order.

Time modifiers: in a player label, the number before the arrow is the actual answer time and the number after it is the time modified by the character. Modified time is used only to find the fastest correct answer to that question. Combined round time uses the numerical-estimate time and the actual times spent answering other players' questions; a questioner's own-question time is excluded. A wrong answer after using Time Freeze adds a {{VS_WRONG_ANSWER_TIME_PENALTY_SECONDS}} second penalty to the combined round time. Character XP may also be awarded by placement at the end of the round.
Vitality: characters get tired during normal rounds. A character loses 1 vitality when it is their turn to ask a question. They lose another 1 vitality after an incorrect answer to another player's question; skipping an answer does not reduce vitality. If a character's vitality reaches zero during a question, they may finish that question. From the next normal question onward, they cannot answer or use help. The numerical estimate and the captain round do not consume vitality. In a four-player match, a character can lose at most 4 vitality in one normal round: 1 for their own question and another 1 for each incorrect answer to the other three questions.

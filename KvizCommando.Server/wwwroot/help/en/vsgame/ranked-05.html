Normal-round flow

A normal round consists of one numerical estimate and one multiple-choice question per player.

1. Numerical estimate – {{VS_GUESS_SECONDS}} secThe closest estimate earns +{{VS_POINT_UNIT}} point. Equally close estimates are ordered by time. This sets the order of questioners.

2. Questions – {{VS_QUESTION_SECONDS}} secEveryone asks once from their assigned category. Submitted answers are final.
Responder: correct +{{VS_POINT_UNIT}}, wrong −{{VS_POINT_UNIT}}, no answer 0 points.
Speed: the uniquely fastest correct responder earns +{{VS_POINT_UNIT}} point . If the questioner achieves the best time , or the best time is tied, the point goes into the shared pool. No correct answer means no pool increase.
Questioner: each opponent adds to the question value: wrong +{{VS_POINT_UNIT}}, skipped +2, correct 0. Your correct answer earns that value; a wrong answer loses it. Skipping costs {{VS_POINT_UNIT}} point per opponent.
End of round: the round winner earns +{{VS_POINT_UNIT}} point; tied scores are settled by shorter round time. The fastest round time receives the shared speed pool, which starts at {{VS_POINT_UNIT}} point.

Timing: round time includes the estimate and the accounted time for every question, including your own. Correct answers use actual time; wrong or skipped answers use {{VS_QUESTION_SECONDS}} seconds. Speed uses modified time, clamped to zero and rounded to tenths of a second.
Helps: the questioner cannot use active help. The 50–50 and AI helps can be used once per round; Time Freeze can be used for multiple questions. With Time Freeze, a correct answer has zero speed time; a wrong answer adds another {{VS_WRONG_ANSWER_TIME_PENALTY_SECONDS}} seconds to round time.
Vitality: asking costs 1 point; a wrong answer to another player’s question costs 1 more. Skipping costs none. You may finish a question after reaching zero vitality, but cannot answer or use help from the next normal question onward. Estimates and the captain round consume no vitality.

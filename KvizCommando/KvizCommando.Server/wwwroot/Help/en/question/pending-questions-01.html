How many submitted questions can you have?

This screen lets you follow questions submitted for review. The number of pending-question slots increases with your team level.

The pair of numbers in the header means: occupied slots / all slots currently available.
Lvl-00 – Lvl-051
Lvl-06 – Lvl-112
Lvl-12 – Lvl-143
Lvl-15 – Lvl-174
Lvl-18+5
PendingWaiting for review; no action is available yet.
ApprovedCan be moved to User questions or deleted.
RejectedThe feedback can be viewed, then the question can be deleted.
NoneAn empty pending-question slot.

! The flashing exclamation mark means the review has finished and an action is available.

How many loadout rows do you have?

Your Loadout determines the topics from which you can receive questions. You can choose one topic in each row.

The number of rows increases with your team level. The screen shows only the rows currently available to you.
Lvl-00 – Lvl-056loadout rows
Lvl-06 – Lvl-088loadout rows
Lvl-09+10loadout rows – this is the maximum

Clicking and changing a topic does not save it automatically. You must save the completed loadout separately.

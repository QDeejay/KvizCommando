LOADOUT // 02 / 03

When can you choose Own question?

If you already have accepted questions of your own, they can also be added to the loadout. Rows marked with the person icon show where the Own question option is available.

1
Only in the upper rowsOwn question cannot be placed in the second half of the loadout.
2
You need your own questionsYou can use no more than the number of accepted own questions you have.
3
Half the loadout is the maximumOwn question can never occupy more than half of the loadout.
SIMPLE EXAMPLE You have 8 loadout rows and 3 accepted questions of your own. You can then choose Own question in at most 3 rows.

If you later delete one of your own questions and this leaves too many Own question entries in the loadout, the excess choice is cleared automatically.
